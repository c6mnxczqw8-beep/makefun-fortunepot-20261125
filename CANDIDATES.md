# Houseplant fortune candidates (draft pass)

One fortune each, at most 120 characters: kind and funny,
for a houseplant, judged by a gardener.

1. Six inches: "You will be admired by a guest, watered by
   a guilty owner, and moved six inches toward destiny." --
   the plant-scale promotion is lovely; the winner below
   adds the resolutions joke, which gardeners feel in their
   bones.
2. Big window: "You will outlive three of your owner's
   resolutions, be watered with renewed commitment, and
   finally get the big window." -- longevity, guilt, and
   real estate: a full fortune at plant scale.
3. Rain: "Great fortune approaches from the left, carrying
   a watering can and an apology for last month." -- sweet.
4. New leaf: "A new leaf is coming. So is a bigger pot.
   Nobody will say which first; grow anyway." -- encouraging.
5. Sun: "The sun will find you at 4 p.m. daily, and the cat
   will pretend it was her idea." -- domestic warmth.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 2. A fortune should prophesy a whole life,
and candidate 2 covers the plant's three great themes:
endurance (outliving three of the owner's resolutions),
devotion renewed by guilt (the committed watering), and
advancement (the big window -- the corner office of the
sill). Candidate 1's six inches is the sweeter single image;
candidate 2 is the complete fortune.
